## Case Study

This project focused on transforming the transactional UI of LITHOS Travel Agency into a high-end hospitality experience. The core objective was to move away from quick-scanning templates and instead design a curated brand narrative that reflects the actual premium nature of the experiences being sold.

# Problem

Most travel agency sites are built for quick scanning, relying on aggressive "book" buttons, immediate prices, and upfront packages. If packages appear right at the top, the site feels like a standard Booking.com listing rather than a beautifully curated experience, failing to build the user's desire before asking for a conversion.

# Strategy

The strategy intentionally avoided aggressive sales tactics in favor of designing for a "slower pace". The page flow was deliberately structured to prioritize the emotional narrative before listing functional details, sequencing the content as: Emotion → Destination → Offer → Social Proof → Properties → Closing. By aligning the narrative arc chronologically with user intent, the design successfully lightens the cognitive load.

# Design

The design relies on photography and typography for visual heavy lifting, utilizing whitespace as a "visual breather" to let high-quality visuals speak for themselves without loud UI clutter. To avoid a standard "landing page template" feel, a different grid was assigned to each section, including full-bleed heroes, asymmetrical photos, and tight galleries. For the accommodation section, a pinned scrolling strategy was implemented where full-width cards stack dynamically over each other with pure movement (no blurring or scaling) to force a slower pace. Finally, a full-image Call to Action was placed just before the footer to leave an immersive impression.

# Results

The redesigned interface successfully forces a slower, more deliberate user pace. By giving each property the entire viewport during the pinned scroll, the design creates a cinematic and expensive feel. Ultimately, the aesthetic structure elevates the brand, focusing on storytelling and aesthetic pleasure over transactional repetition.
