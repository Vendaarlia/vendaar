## Case Study

This project was created as a collaboration between **vendaar.top** and **Lordicon.com** to explore one thing: how **Lordicon’s Doodle icon collection** can support a **typography-first landing page** without turning into decoration or competing with the type.

As the “playground”, I used **Architects Daughter** (a handwritten typeface) and designed a landing page concept that shifts the typical font page format (specimen, technical info, download button) into a more story-led experience—where icons help guide meaning, structure, and reading flow.

# Problem

Most font websites explain. Very few inspire. The standard format — specimen text, technical details, download button — treats typography as a product to be listed rather than an experience to be felt. For a typeface as characterful as Architects Daughter, that approach leaves most of its personality on the table.

# Strategy

The strategy was built around a single tension: precision vs. human expression. Blueprint grids and Swiss editorial layouts on one side. Handwritten typography, sketch illustrations, and natural imperfections on the other. The goal was to let both coexist without one overpowering the other — and to make the Lordicon Doodle icons feel like a natural part of that visual language, not decorative additions placed on top of it.

# Design

The design direction treated the grid as a blueprint — structured, measured, deliberate — while the typography and illustrations pushed against it with warmth and imperfection. Whitespace was used editorially, giving each section room to breathe rather than filling the page with content. The Lordicon icons were integrated with purpose: every icon supports the content and reinforces meaning. Icons shouldn't compete with typography. They should support it. The result moves from a static font specimen into a story-led experience — from font to experience.

# Results

The concept successfully demonstrates that a font landing page can function as a brand narrative rather than a technical document. By balancing precision and human expression, the design gives Architects Daughter a visual world that reflects its actual character — structured enough to feel considered, imperfect enough to feel alive. Featured by Lordicon on their Instagram and Facebook as part of their official collaboration showcase.
